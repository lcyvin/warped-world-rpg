# Responsible Roleplaying

Tabletop Roleplaying Games allow you to play mystical beasts, shapeshifting thieves, 
and every day humans from a myriad of backgrounds. With this freedom comes some
responsibility. If you as a player choose to play a character whose background does
not match your own, be mindful of the kind of marginalization people who come from 
those backgrounds face, and consider how you roleplay your character in response to 
that. Caricature and stereotype of a background you are not a part of can often be
alienating and hostile. You may as a group decide that certain backgrounds are 
off-limits to players who haven’t experienced them, be that disability, gender identity,
or race.
